# Atlaswild fact review

Checked 2026-11-01. Covers `src/content/us/{west,midwest,south,northeast}.json` and `src/content/europe/countries.json`: 94 records (50 states, 44 countries), 188 facts and 188 questions.

Result: 64 text changes across 34 records (27 facts, 34 questions, 3 hints) and 9 nickname status changes. No capital, city list or largest city needed changing.

**How far this goes.** About 60 claims were looked up on the web, including everything in the section below. The rest (most capitals, the same-place city lists, and well-known history such as "Delaware ratified first") were checked from general knowledge only and were not individually looked up. Treat those as reviewed, not sourced.

## Could not confirm, or for a parent to decide

- **Florida "Sunshine State".** Several secondary sources say the Legislature adopted it in 1970, but it is not in the Florida Statutes and no state source could be opened. It is probably real; a state page would justify setting it back to `official`.
- **Statute text.** Arkansas, Hawaii, Arizona and California were read through legal mirrors rather than the state's own site.
- **Statue of Liberty.** The island's state is legally odd: it sits in New Jersey waters, is usually described as an exclave of New York, and sources differ on what the 1834 compact and the 1998 Ellis Island case mean for it. The question was rewritten to avoid the issue.
- **Hells Canyon.** The US Forest Service page could not be opened, and rival claims (Copper Canyon, Kings Canyon) could not be settled, so "deepest in North America" was dropped.
- **Tara Canyon.** Some sources call it the deepest in continental Europe, depending on where Europe ends. "One of the deepest" is true either way.
- **Lake counts** depend on the size cutoff. A database that counts only lakes over 0.1 square km gives Belarus 1,420.
- **Close population races** that could flip with the next estimates: Charleston and Huntington WV (46,117 to 44,815), Charleston and Columbia SC, Bridgeport and Stamford CT, Cheyenne and Casper WY.
- **Largest cities outside the US** are from general knowledge except the four tiny countries above. Belgium counts Antwerp as largest by city limits; greater Brussels is bigger.
- **Gulf of Mexico.** Kept in the Louisiana question. The US government now uses "Gulf of America"; the name was not researched here. Worth a family decision.
- **Bern** is Switzerland's seat of government, but Swiss law does not formally name a capital (general knowledge, not looked up).
- **Moldova's language.** Romanian and Moldovan are the same language, but people label it differently and the topic is political.
- **Kosovo, Cyprus and Turkey** are not in the game, so neighbor lists for Serbia, Albania, Montenegro and North Macedonia leave out Kosovo.
- **Monaco and San Marino** "cities" are quarters and municipalities, not separate cities.
