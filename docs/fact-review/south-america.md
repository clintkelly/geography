# South America fact review

Checked 2026-11-01. Covers `src/content/world/south-america.json`: 12 records (all 12 UN member states), 24 facts and 24 questions. French Guiana and the Falkland Islands are left out, so neither appears as a neighbor. Every country has a land border with at least one other, so no flight or sea links were needed.

**How far this goes.** The claims below were looked up on the web. Most came through search results or page summaries, not a full read of each source page. Items under "General knowledge only" were not individually looked up. Treat those as reviewed, not sourced.

## For the owner to decide

1. **Bolivia's capital: this file uses Sucre.** It is the capital named in the constitution. Many maps and quiz sites give La Paz, so a child may see a different answer elsewhere. One of Bolivia's facts says so plainly: "Sucre is the capital in Bolivia's constitution, but the president and congress work in La Paz." To switch, change `capital`, that fact and its question.
2. **La Paz is left out of Bolivia's `sameStateCities` on purpose.** Otherwise the game would say "La Paz is a city in Bolivia, but not its capital", which would mislead. Potosí takes its place.
3. **Ecuador's largest city** is close and could flip at the next census. It is Guayaquil on 2022 figures.
4. **Paraguay and Venezuela largest city** depend on the boundary used. Asunción is first by city limits. Caracas is first on the source's metro basis.
5. **Brazil has a `hint2`** beyond the rules, because "Brasília" nearly spells the country's name in clue 2. Remove it if you prefer the plain capital clue.
6. **Guyana and Venezuela** are listed as neighbors with no mention of their border dispute, as the brief requires.
7. **All 12 `nickname` values are null.** None seemed both widely used and well sourced.
