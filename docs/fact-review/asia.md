# Asia fact review

Checked 2026-11-01. Covers `src/content/world/asia.json`: 48 records (47 UN member states, plus UN observer Palestine), 96 facts and 96 questions. Russia stays in the Europe file. Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macao are not added.

**How far this goes.** The claims in the tables below were looked up on the web. Most came from Wikipedia, and many were read through a summarizing fetch tool rather than line by line. UNESCO's own pages (whc.unesco.org) refused the fetch, so UNESCO claims rest on Wikipedia or news reports. Items in "General knowledge only" were not looked up.

## For the owner to decide

- **Israel's capital.** Set to Jerusalem. The sources do not say so without a qualifier. Wikipedia's infobox says "Jerusalem (limited recognition)" and calls it the "government seat and proclaimed capital". The UN table lists it with the footnote "Designation and data provided by Israel. The position of the United Nations on the question of Jerusalem is contained in General Assembly resolution 181(II) and subsequent resolutions". The game will state flatly "Jerusalem is the capital of Israel."
- **Palestine's capital.** Set to East Jerusalem, because that is what the UN table prints, with the footnote "Designation and data provided by the State of Palestine" and the same line on the UN's position. It is a designation the UN reports, not one it endorses. Ramallah is the seat of government and the usual alternative (Wikipedia: "Ramallah (de facto)"). The game will state flatly "East Jerusalem is the capital of Palestine." No UN capital page could be opened: UNdata's country pages did not load, so this rests on one table. If you would rather not state a capital for either, the templates would need a change; `cityState` is not the right switch.
- **Israel and Palestine in one question.** "Jerusalem" and "East Jerusalem" could confuse children, and the `near` options will often put these two side by side.
- **Calling Palestine a country.** The game's noun is "country", so it will say "capital of which country?". The two fact questions avoid this by asking "Where is...". Its map shape includes Gaza and the West Bank.
- **Palestine's largest city.** Gaza, by the last census and the UN's 2021 figure, but those numbers predate the war. The game would say "Gaza is the largest city in Palestine." The rule requires the capital or a listed city, and Hebron would be wrong by those figures.
- **Turkey or Türkiye.** The name is Turkey as asked. The UN has used Türkiye since 2022 (general knowledge, not looked up).
- **Cyprus.** The map has a separate shape for N. Cyprus with no id, so the north is not clickable. `lonLat` is in the south, and Nicosia is a divided city. Cyprus's neighbors are Lebanon and Israel: direct flights from Turkey to the Republic of Cyprus do not exist (general knowledge).
- **Closed borders.** Armenia-Turkey, Armenia-Azerbaijan and the two Koreas are neighbors in the graph because they border, not because the crossings are open.
- **Syria's largest city.** Aleppo rests on pre-war figures. Damascus may be larger now.
- **Socotra.** Administratively part of Yemen, though control is contested. The fact stays with trees.
- **Dated claims to recheck:** the Kuwaiti dinar, Rain Vortex, Petronas Towers, Fedchenko Glacier, and Indonesia's capital if a decree is signed.
- **Taiwan** has a shape on the map and is not in the game. Its neighbors are therefore not listed.
- **Sri Lanka's capital** has a long label that may not fit the answer buttons.

## Owner decisions

- Israel is a country like any other, with Jerusalem stated as its capital. This overrides the discussion of Israel's capital above.
- Palestine's capital is Ramallah, its seat of government, replacing East Jerusalem.
- Taiwan is included as a country. Capital Taipei and New Taipei as largest city were confirmed by search; Taipei 101's 2004 title and bubble tea's 1980s origin come from search summaries.
