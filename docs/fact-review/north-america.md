# North America fact review

Checked 2026-11-01. Covers `src/content/world/north-america.json`: 23 records (all 23 sovereign states of North America), 46 facts, 46 questions.

**How far this goes.** Every superlative, ranking and number in the facts was looked up, along with all capitals and the claims below. Most lookups were web searches read through their result summaries, not full readings of each page, so the URLs below are where the finding came from but were mostly not opened in full. No fact was dropped for lack of a source; several were softened where sources disagreed (see below).

## Machine checks

`fips` values come from `node_modules/world-atlas/countries-50m.json`; all 23 are present. `lonLat` was tested with `geoContains` against each country's map shape. `tiny` was set from projected size on a 960x500 Natural Earth map: everything under about 10 square pixels.

## For the owner to decide

1. **Trinidad and Tobago's largest city.** The file uses Chaguanas, the census answer. The game will say "Chaguanas is the largest city in Trinidad and Tobago." A family using metro areas might expect Port of Spain.
2. **Honduras's capital.** The file uses Tegucigalpa, the usual answer. Comayagüela is constitutionally co-official.
3. **Soup joumou.** It celebrates Haitian independence and was once forbidden to enslaved people. The fact leaves that out on purpose, under the no-slavery rule, so it reads as a food fact only. A parent may want to add the history in person.
4. **`tiny` borderline cases.** Bahamas, Jamaica and Trinidad and Tobago are marked tiny (areas 9, 7.6 and 3.4 square pixels). El Salvador (about 14) is not. Trial it on the real map.
5. **Look-alike wrong answers.** Kingston (Jamaica) and Kingstown (Saint Vincent) are both capitals, and Nicaragua lists the city of Granada next to the country Grenada. These may trip children, which could be good or annoying.
6. **Santiago.** The Dominican Republic's city is listed as "Santiago", short for Santiago de los Caballeros. Panama also lists a Santiago.
7. **Loose neighbor links.** Dominican Republic to Antigua and Barbuda, and Panama to Jamaica, exist mainly to keep the map connected.
